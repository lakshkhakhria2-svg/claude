FAQ

Questions founders ask.

What does a site cost?
Launch pages start at $600 and full startup sites at $1,500. After a 20-minute call you get a fixed quote within 24 hours, and that's the price you pay.
How fast can you launch?
A launch page takes about a week. A full marketing site usually takes 2–3 weeks, depending on how quickly copy and feedback come back.
Will all this animation slow my site down?
No. Motion is added on top of a fast, static page, animates only transforms and opacity, and turns off for visitors who ask their device for less motion. Every build is checked against a 90+ Lighthouse mobile score.
Do I own the code?
Yes. You get the full source, the design system and a walkthrough video. Host it anywhere.
What do you need from me?
Your goal, your audience and any brand assets you already have. If you don't have copy yet, I'll structure it with you.
