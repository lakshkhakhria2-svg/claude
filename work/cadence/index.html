Questions, answered.

Does it replace our standup completely?
For most teams, yes. Some keep one short call a week for planning and use Cadence for the daily updates.
Where do check-ins happen?
In Slack or Microsoft Teams. Nobody needs to open another app.
Can managers see who skipped?
Completion is shown per team, not per person, unless your workspace turns individual tracking on.
What happens after the trial?
You drop back to the free Starter plan unless you choose to upgrade. Nothing is deleted.
