Good questions.

Do I need to be home?
No. Most customers leave a key in a lockbox. Your cleaner texts when they arrive and when they leave.

Yes, all products and equipment are included in the price. Tell us if you'd prefer we use yours.
What if I need to cancel?
Cancel or move a visit for free up to 24 hours before. No contracts on regular cleans.
Is the re-clean really free?
Yes. Tell us within 48 hours and we'll come back to fix anything that was missed, at no cost.
